How it works

  1. Add your accounts with what is in them today: bank, cash, cards.
  2. Assign that money to categories (rent, groceries, savings) until nothing is left to assign.
  3. Record what you spend, or import your bank's CSV file. Each category shows what is still available.
  4. Overspent? Move money from another category. The budget always adds up.

Your budget is saved in this browser only. Download a backup file now and then (Settings) to keep it safe or move it to another device.
