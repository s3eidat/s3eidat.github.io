A free link in bio page that you own

A link in bio page puts all your links behind the one address you can add to Instagram, TikTok or YouTube. If you want something like Linktree but without the vendor's branding on a free page, without an account and without your page living on someone else's site, open Link Page. You design it with a live phone preview, then download it as one file and put it online for free.

Open Link Page

Step by step

  1. Open Link Page. It starts with a Creator example; the menu at the top left has a blank page and Small shop, Musician and Personal templates.
  2. In Content, add your photo, name and a short bio.
  3. Add your links: a button text and an address each. Use + Header to group them, Paste links to add a whole list at once, and the ⋯ menu to highlight a link or turn it into a WhatsApp, email or call link.
  4. Add social icons: pick the network and type your username, or paste the link.
  5. In Design, pick one of 12 themes, or set your own background (colour, gradient or photo), button style, colours and font.
  6. In Publish, check the list, press Download index.html, and follow the steps for the free host you like. Then paste your new address into your profile.
  7. Type the address under QR code to download a QR code for posters, business cards or your shop window.

Who it helps

  • Creators. Latest video, shop and brand contact in one place, on an address no one can take away.
  • Small shops and home businesses. Order on WhatsApp with a ready-made message, price list, location and opening hours.
  • Musicians and artists. New release, tour dates, merch and booking.
  • Students and job seekers. Portfolio, CV and LinkedIn behind one link you can print as a QR code.

Why it's private

Link Page is a static page. What you type and the photos you add stay in your browser's storage on this device and are never uploaded to us. The page's security policy blocks it from sending data to other websites, and it works offline after the first visit. The page you download has no scripts, no trackers and no ads: it shows your links and nothing else. When you upload it to a host, that host's own terms apply.

Tips and limits

  • The preview is the real thing: what you see on the phone is exactly the file you download.
  • Links without an address are shown in the preview but left out of the download; Publish lists them.
  • Photos are built into the file. Your profile photo is cropped square, and background photos are resized to 1600 pixels, so the file stays small.
  • Contrast hints warn you when text would be hard to read on your colours.
  • Not included: hosting, click statistics, email sign-up forms, payments or scheduled links. Changes mean downloading and uploading the file again.

FAQ

Do you host my page?

No. You download your page as one file and put it on a free host such as Netlify, GitHub Pages or Cloudflare Pages, or on your own website. The app shows the steps for each. Because the page is yours, nobody can switch it off except you and your host.

Is there any branding on my page?

No. There is an optional small line at the bottom that you can turn on if you want to; it is off by default.

Can I see how many people click my links?

Not from this tool. The page has no scripts and no trackers, so it collects nothing. Some hosts offer their own visitor statistics you can switch on.

How do I change the page later?

Your page stays saved in this browser. Open the tool, make your changes, download index.html again and upload it to your host in place of the old one. Use Save file to keep a copy you can open on another device.

Can I make the page in Arabic?

Yes. Names, bio and buttons can be in Arabic or any language, and you can set the whole page to right-to-left in Design.
