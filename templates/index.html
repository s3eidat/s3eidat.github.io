Questions

Do I need hosting?

Any host works: GitHub Pages and Netlify are free. The README walks you through it.

Can I change the colours and videos?

Yes. Colours are one line at the top of the file, and you can swap the videos for your own footage.

How does the done-for-you service work?

After paying you fill in a short form (name, services, phone, links). You get a preview link within 48 hours and one round of changes, then it goes live on your domain. Your first year of domain and hosting is included; after that it's $59/year, and you can cancel any time by replying to your receipt email.

Refunds?

Templates are digital downloads, so all sales are final, but if something doesn't work we'll fix it. For the done-for-you service, if we haven't started your site yet you can get a full refund.
