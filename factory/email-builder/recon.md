# Recon: Email Builder (free alternative to Mailchimp's email designer)

Light recon, public pages only (run 2026-10-29). No Mailchimp code, templates, illustrations, icons or copy reused.

## What the paid app is
Email marketing platform; its drag-and-drop email designer builds newsletters from blocks (text, image, button,
columns, divider, social, footer), previews desktop/mobile and can export HTML. Sending, lists and automation are
the paid platform around it.
Sources: https://www.sender.net/reviews/mailchimp/pricing/free-plan , https://dreamlit.ai/blog/mailchimp-review ,
https://support.itgalleryapp.com/article/323-exporting-templates-from-mailchimp

## Pricing
Free plan with small contact/send caps and one user; paid plans by contacts. Standalone template builders cap free
HTML exports (e.g. 4 exports a month on some free tiers: https://www.sequenzy.com/blog/best-free-html-email-builders).
Guide copy: "paid plans", "export limits", no exact prices.

## What users complain about
- Templates are tied to the platform; exporting drag-and-drop templates is limited or warned against
  (https://jump.wordstream.com/mailchimp-export-html).
- Free tiers of template tools cap exports per month (sequenzy link above).
- Need an account just to design one email.

## Our angle
Build an email from blocks in the browser and export clean, email-safe HTML (tables, inline styles, mobile-friendly)
as many times as you like, to paste into Gmail, Outlook, Mailchimp, Brevo, MailerLite or any tool. No account.

## Honest limits (do not claim)
- It does not send email or manage lists.
- Images must be on the web (paste their address) to show in most inboxes; uploaded images are embedded as data and
  many email apps block those.
- Email apps differ; we test structure, not every client (no Litmus-style screenshots).
