# Recon: PDF Editor (free alternative to Adobe Acrobat)

Light recon, public pages only (run 2026-10-29). No Adobe code/branding/copy to be reused.

## What the paid app is
Acrobat: view, edit text/images, annotate, fill & sign forms, organise pages, merge/split, convert, protect, OCR, AI assistant.

## Pricing (third-party summaries, vary; verify on Adobe's page before quoting any number in guides)
- Pro ~ $19.99/mo on annual plan, ~ $29.99/mo month-to-month; Standard ~ $12.99-14.99/mo annual. AI Assistant add-on ~ $4.99/mo.
  Sources: https://photutorial.com/adobe-acrobat-pricing , https://redresscompliance.com/adobe-acrobat-pro-current-pricing-2026 , https://thebusinessdive.com/adobe-acrobat-pricing
- Guide copy rule: say "subscription" and "paid plans", avoid exact prices.

## What users complain about
- Editing needs a paid monthly plan; even "just edit a PDF" confuses people (Adobe Community: https://community.adobe.com/t5/acrobat-reader-discussions/acrobat-reader/td-p/9041971).
- Aggressive upselling and hard cancellation (https://www.joinsecret.com/adobe-acrobat-reader/reviews).
- Free web alternatives cap usage (e.g. Sejda: 3 tasks/day, 200 pages) or watermark output (PDF-XChange); files get uploaded to servers (https://blog.flipsnack.com/edit-pdf-without-acrobat-adobe/ , https://www.ionos.com/digitalguide/websites/web-design/alternatives-to-adobe-acrobat.md).
- Press: "XDA stops paying for Acrobat after browser discovery" (https://digg.com/tech/98rd3qbx) - browsers/free tools cover most needs.

## Our angle
Free, no limits, no watermark, no sign-up, files never leave the device (no network at all), works offline after load.

## Honest limits (do not claim)
- No true re-flow editing of existing PDF text (browser-feasible: cover-and-replace is hacky). We add new text boxes, whiteout, annotations.
- No OCR, no cryptographic digital signatures (only visual/drawn signature), no PDF/A, no redaction guarantees unless we flatten (could: true redaction by rasterising page - "could").
- Encrypted PDFs: password open maybe via pdf.js only.
