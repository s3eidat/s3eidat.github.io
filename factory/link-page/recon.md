# Recon: Link Page (free alternative to Linktree)

Light recon, public pages and reviews only (run 2026-10-29). Nothing from Linktree (code, logo, colours, tree
icon, templates, copy) is reused. Generic terms only (link in bio, link page, profile, buttons, social icons).

## What the paid app is
A hosted "link in bio" page: avatar, name, short bio, a stack of link buttons, a row of social icons, themes
(backgrounds, button shapes, fonts), link scheduling, click analytics, email/SMS collection, commerce and
"shop" blocks, a QR code for the page. Pages live on the vendor's domain (linktr.ee/<name>).

## Pricing and limits (secondary sources; check the vendor's pricing page before quoting a number)
- Free plan is permanent with unlimited links and basic analytics; removing the vendor's branding, deeper
  analytics, scheduling and full theme control need a paid plan. Paid tiers are quoted at roughly $5–$35 per
  month depending on source and billing period. Sources:
  https://app.unilink.us/blog/linktree-pricing-2026 , https://u2l.ai/blog/linktree-pricing ,
  https://linkie.bio/blog/is-linktree-free , https://toolradar.com/tools/linktree/pricing
- Guide copy rule: say "the free plan shows the vendor's branding and keeps some styling behind a paid plan";
  avoid exact prices.

## What users complain about
- Accounts removed or suspended with little explanation; the creator's link stops working overnight
  (https://www.trustpilot.com/review/linktr.ee?page=6 , https://www.capterra.com/p/229171/Linktree/reviews/?page=3 ,
  https://www.inverse.com/input/tech/linktree-just-banned-tons-of-sex-workers-overnight).
- Branding on the free page and basic customisation behind a subscription
  (https://app.unilink.us/blog/is-linktree-free).
- Renewal/billing surprises (Trustpilot pages above).
- The page lives on someone else's domain, so you don't own the link.

## Our angle
Design the page in the browser with a live phone preview, then download ONE self-contained HTML file (images
embedded, no scripts, no trackers, no branding) and host it anywhere for free (GitHub Pages, Netlify Drop,
Cloudflare Pages, or your own site). Nobody can take it down but you and your host; you can move it any time.
QR code for the final address, step-by-step hosting instructions.

## Honest limits (do not claim)
- We do not host the page; the user puts the file on a free host (we explain how). No vanity linktr.ee-style URL.
- No click analytics, email collection, payments, scheduling or link-level stats (no server). Hosts may offer
  their own analytics; we don't promise any.
- Changing the page means editing here and uploading the new file again.
