# Recon: Subtitle Editor (free alternative to Kapwing's subtitle tools)

Light recon, public pages only (run 2026-10-29). No Kapwing code, branding, styles or copy reused.

## What the paid app is
Online video editor with a subtitle editor: auto-subtitles, edit lines on a timeline synced to playback, styles,
import/export SRT/VTT, burn captions into the exported video, translation.
Sources: https://www.kapwing.com/help/is-kapwing-free/ , https://www.kapwing.com/help/subscription-faq-2/ ,
https://morphed.app/blog/kapwing-free-plan-limits

## Pricing
Free plan with a watermark on exports, short export length and upload size cap; Pro/Business per member per month
(official help pages above; third-party numbers vary). Guide copy rule: "paid plans", no exact prices.

## What users complain about
- Prominent watermark on free exports (Gartner Peer Insights review:
  https://gcom.pdo.aws.gartner.com/reviews/product/kapwing).
- Short free export length and 250 MB upload cap; monthly subtitle-minute caps
  (https://www.kapwing.com/help/is-kapwing-free/ , https://fluxnote.io/guides/kapwing-free-plan-limits-2026).
- Videos must be uploaded to their servers.

## Our angle
Free, no watermark, no length or size cap beyond your device, video never uploaded. Edit lines on a timeline synced
to playback, import/export SRT and VTT, style captions, burn them into a new video. For automatic subtitles, link to
our own /transcribe/ tool (also local) and import its SRT.

## Honest limits (do not claim)
- No speech recognition inside this tool (use /transcribe/, then import the SRT).
- Burn-in export records in real time (a 5-minute video takes about 5 minutes) and makes WebM (MP4 in Safari);
  quality depends on the browser's encoder.
- Very long or huge files may be too heavy for the waveform on phones.
