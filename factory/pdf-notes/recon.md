# Recon: PDF Notes (free alternative to GoodNotes)

Light recon, public pages only (run 2026-10-29). No GoodNotes code, templates, covers, icons or copy reused.

## What the paid app is
Handwritten notes on iPad (and now Windows/Android/web): notebooks with paper templates, import PDFs (lecture
slides, worksheets) and write on them with pen/highlighter, eraser, lasso, shapes, search, sync, AI features.
Sources: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/goodnotes-6-ai-notes-docs/id1444383602 ,
https://techcrunch.com/2023/08/09/goodnotes-biggest-update-in-four-years-brings-ai-powered-handwriting-features-and-a-digital-marketplace ,
https://brainbound.blog/goodnotes-price-guide

## Pricing
Free tier limited to three notebooks; yearly subscription or one-time purchase (Apple only); account required
(sources above; prices vary by region). Guide copy: "paid plans / subscription", no exact prices.

## What users complain about
- Three-notebook cap on the free tier; existing notebooks above the cap became read-only after the upgrade
  (https://www.malaymail.com/news/tech-gadgets/2023/08/24/is-it-all-good-with-the-updated-goodnotes-6/87000).
- Account registration and subscriptions required (same + https://landian.news/?p=763).
- Users who paid for the old app feel pushed into paying again (r/GoodNotes thread via
  https://redlib.hackliberty.org/r/GoodNotes/comments/1gtmvx5/what_happened_to_goodnotes).

## Our angle
Unlimited notebooks, no account, works in any browser with a pen, finger or mouse (iPad, Android tablet, Windows
touch laptop, Chromebook). Import a PDF and write on it, or start a blank/lined/grid/dotted notebook. Export the
annotated PDF. Files never leave the device (IndexedDB).

## Honest limits (do not claim)
- No sync between devices (export the PDF or a backup instead), no handwriting search/OCR, no AI.
- Strokes are exported to the PDF as vector lines with even thickness (pressure shows on screen).
- Very large PDFs (hundreds of pages) depend on the device's memory.
