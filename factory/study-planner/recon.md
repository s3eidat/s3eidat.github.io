# Recon: Study Planner (free alternative to MyStudyLife)

Light recon, public pages only (run 2026-10-29). No MyStudyLife code, branding, icons or copy to be reused.

## What the paid app is
Student planner: subjects/classes timetable (weekly or rotating 2–4 week / day-cycle schedules), tasks
(homework, reminders, revision) with due dates, exams with date/time/room, dashboard of "today", reminders,
cloud sync across phone/web. Premium tier (MyStudyLife+) adds subtasks, grade tracking, personalisation,
"AI schedule scan" and (depending on listing) a Pomodoro timer.
Sources: https://mystudylife.com/ , https://care.mystudylife.com/en/articles/9502657-how-to-use-msl ,
https://apps.apple.com/za/app/mystudylife-study-planner/id910639339

## Pricing
Free tier + MyStudyLife+ subscription, monthly or yearly, prices vary by region (App Store listings).
Guide copy rule: say "paid plan / subscription", no exact prices.

## What users complain about
- Free tier now shows ads and caps active tasks (vendor comparison, treat with caution):
  https://dormway.app/blog/dormway-vs-mystudylife
- Crashes, bugs and data loss after updates; interface got more confusing; features moved behind the paywall:
  https://www.coursesync.biz/post/mystudylife-reviews-is-it-good-free-and-worth-using-in-2026
- Web version redirects users to the app (AlternativeTo comment): https://alternativeto.net/software/my-study-life/about
- Students asking whether it is shutting down: https://www.coursicle.com/blog/is-my-study-life-shutting-down/
- It records exams but leaves the revision plan to you (you add revision tasks by hand).

## Our angle
Free, no account, no ads tracking you, no task cap, data stays on the device (localStorage + backup file),
works offline. The thing we add: an automatic revision plan from today to each exam date, spread over the
time you say you have each day, re-planned when you miss a session. English + Arabic UI.

## Honest limits (do not claim)
- No cloud sync between devices: use the backup file (export/import) or the .ics calendar export.
- No push notifications when the page is closed; no LMS (Canvas/Moodle) integration; no AI timetable scan.
- No grade tracking (link to /gpa-calc/ instead).
