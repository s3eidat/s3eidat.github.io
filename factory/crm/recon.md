# Recon: CRM (free alternative to HubSpot CRM)

Light recon, public pages only (run 2026-10-29). No HubSpot code, branding, icons or copy reused.

## What the paid app is
CRM: contacts and companies, deals in a pipeline board with stages and amounts, tasks and reminders, an activity
timeline (notes, calls, emails, meetings), dashboards, imports, plus marketing/sales/service hubs on top.
Sources: https://www.impactplus.com/blog/is-the-free-hubspot-crm-actually-free , https://blog.close.com/hubspot-crm-limitations/ ,
https://www.nutshell.com/blog/hubspot-free-crm

## Pricing
Free CRM with limits (users, contacts, branding, no automation in free), then Starter, then a very large jump to
Professional (sources above; numbers vary and several are competitors). Guide copy: "paid tiers", no exact prices.

## What users complain about
- The price jump from Starter to Professional (HubSpot Community thread:
  https://community.hubspot.com/t5/CRM/Create-competitive-pricing-for-small-companies/m-p/619782/highlight/true).
- Free tier limits and upsells; billing disputes and contract rigidity in reviews (https://startupowl.com/reviews/hubspot).
- Customer data lives on a US company's servers (a concern for small businesses in other regions).

## Our angle
A small-business CRM that is free with no limits we impose, keeps every record on the device (backup file +
CSV), works offline, supports JOD and other currencies. Pipeline board, contacts, companies, tasks, timeline,
dashboard, CSV import/export.

## Honest limits (do not claim)
- Single user, one device: no team sharing, no sync (use the backup file).
- No email sending/tracking, no automation/workflows, no calendar sync. Email and phone are links that open your
  own apps.
