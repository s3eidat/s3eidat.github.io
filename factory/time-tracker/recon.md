# Recon: Time Tracker (free alternative to Toggl Track)

Light recon, public pages only (run 2026-10-29). Nothing from Toggl (code, logo, colours, illustrations, copy) is
reused. Generic time-tracking terms only (timer, project, client, tag, billable, timesheet, report).

## What the paid app is
One-click timer with description, project, tags and billable flag; manual entries; projects and clients; weekly
timesheet; calendar view; reports (summary, detailed, weekly) with charts; billable rates and amounts; exports
(CSV, PDF); browser extension and app integrations; idle detection; team features (approvals, admin).

## Pricing (third-party summaries; they disagree — verify on the vendor page before quoting a number)
- Free plan for a small team (sources say up to 5, some say 3 users). Paid "Starter" around $9–10 per user per
  month and "Premium" around $18–20.
  Sources: https://costbench.com/software/time-tracking/toggl-track/free-plan , https://www.timely.com/blog/toggl-track-pricing/ ,
  https://www.capterra.com/p/247745/Toggl/pricing
- Billable rates, project budgets and revenue reporting are paid-only per several sources:
  https://dupple.com/learn/best-free-time-tracking-software , https://hubstaff.com/blog/toggl-track-review
- Guide copy rule: say "billable rates are on paid plans", avoid exact prices and user caps.

## What users complain about
- Billable rates / amounts and invoicing behind the paywall; free tier too limited for freelancers who bill
  (G2: https://www.g2.com/survey_responses/toggl-track-review-2769503 ; Hubstaff review above;
  Capterra comparison: https://www.capterra.com/compare/212371-247745/Bill4Time-vs-Toggl).
- Per-user price adds up; slow support (competitor review: https://clockify.me/reviews/toggl-track-review).
- Timesheet issues (https://connecteam.com/reviews/toggl-track/).
- Generally liked: the simple one-click timer and clean reports (Capterra reviews:
  https://www.capterra.com/p/247745/Toggl/reviews/).

## Our angle
Free billable rates (client and project), amounts in reports, an invoice you can print or save as PDF, CSV
exports, a weekly timesheet, and import of a Toggl CSV export to switch. No account, nothing uploaded, works
offline, the running timer survives closing the tab. Week can start on Saturday, Sunday or Monday.

## Honest limits (do not claim)
- No team features, approvals, sync between devices (backup file instead), browser extension, integrations,
  idle detection or reminders when the page is closed.
- Entries count toward the day they start on (an entry over midnight is not split).
- Invoice PDF is made through the browser's print dialog ("Save as PDF").
