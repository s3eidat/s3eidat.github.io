# Recon: Storyboard (free alternative to Boords)

Light recon, public pages only (run 2026-10-29). No Boords code, branding, icons, illustrations or copy reused.

## What the paid app is
Online storyboard tool for agencies/filmmakers: frames grid with image (upload, draw, AI images), action/dialogue/
notes fields, reorder, comments and approval links for clients, animatic (frame timing + voiceover), exports:
PDF (covers, layouts, paper sizes), Word, spreadsheet/shot list, images, Google Slides, MP4 animatic.
Sources: https://boords.com/docs/export-formats , https://boords.com/for/filmmakers ,
https://planetnutshell.com/boords-review-heres-why-were-hooked/

## Pricing
Free tier with limits; paid plans per month, team pricing; third-party sources disagree on numbers
(https://www.trustradius.com/products/boords , https://storyflow.so/blog/storyboardhero-vs-boords-2026).
Guide copy rule: say "paid plans", no exact prices.

## What users complain about
- More expensive than people expect for small creators/students (https://storyflow.so/blog/storyboardhero-vs-boords-2026).
- Layout/visual customisation is limited; people make concept art elsewhere and import it (https://www.g2.com/products/boords/reviews).
- Drawing tools are basic compared with illustration apps (https://us.fitgap.com/products/boords).
- Some exports (MP4 animatic) only on higher plans (https://planetnutshell.com/boords-review-heres-why-were-hooked/).
- Needs an account and uploads your frames to their servers (all cloud tools).

## Our angle
Free, no account, frames never leave the device (IndexedDB + project file), works offline. Sketch or drop images,
shot type / camera move / action / dialogue / duration per frame, animatic player, PDF export with no watermark,
animatic video export (WebM) and shot list CSV for free.

## Honest limits (do not claim)
- No collaboration, comments, client approval links or cloud sync (share the PDF or the project file).
- No AI image generation, no voiceover recording in the animatic (could), video export is WebM (not MP4) and
  depends on browser MediaRecorder support.
- PDF pages are rendered images (text is not selectable).
