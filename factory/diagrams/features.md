# Diagrams — features (browser-only)

## Must (core)
- Infinite canvas: pan (drag empty space with Space or middle button, wheel, touch), zoom (Ctrl+wheel, pinch,
  buttons, fit to content).
- Shape library: rectangle, rounded, ellipse, circle, diamond, parallelogram, terminator, document, database
  (cylinder), hexagon, triangle, note, text, entity (ER table), actor.
- Add by click or drag from the library; select, multi-select (Shift, marquee), move, resize, delete,
  duplicate, copy/paste, undo/redo.
- Text in shapes (double-click to edit), wrap, size, bold, align, colour; text on connectors.
- Connectors that follow shapes: drag from a shape's connection dot to another shape; straight, elbow and
  curved; arrowheads at either end; dashed; colour and width.
- Snap to grid and alignment guides to other shapes.
- Colours: fill, line, text; line width and dash.
- Arrange: bring to front / send to back (layer order).
- Templates: flowchart, org chart, ER diagram.
- Export PNG and SVG; save/open .diagram.json file; autosave in this browser.

## Should (full)
- Export PDF (image in PDF); transparent PNG option; export selection only.
- My diagrams list (several diagrams saved in the browser), rename, delete.
- Quick-add: arrows around a selected shape create a connected copy in that direction.
- Align and distribute for multiple shapes; lock; arrow-key nudge; keyboard shortcuts + help.
- Polish: mobile 390px (library strip, bottom properties), light/dark canvas, empty state, S3_SELFTEST,
  offline service worker, Arabic text right to left.

## Could (later)
- Swimlanes, groups, layers panel, Mermaid import, rotation, images on canvas, more shape sets (UML, network).
