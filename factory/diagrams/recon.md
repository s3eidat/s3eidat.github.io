# Recon: Diagrams (free alternative to Lucidchart)

Light recon, public pages only (run 2026-10-29). Nothing from Lucidchart (code, shape art, icons, templates,
colours, copy) is reused; shapes are basic geometry drawn fresh.

## What the paid app is
Browser diagramming: flowcharts, org charts, ER/UML, network diagrams, mind maps, swimlanes. Drag shapes from
a library, connectors that stay attached when shapes move, smart guides/snapping, text, styles, layers,
templates, export (PNG, JPEG, PDF, SVG, Visio), real-time collaboration, data linking, AI generation.

## Pricing (third-party summaries; verify on the vendor page before quoting a number)
- Free plan: 3 editable documents, a cap on shapes per document (reported 60, raised to 75 in June 2026),
  limited templates. Paid individual plan around $9/month.
  Sources: https://costbench.com/software/diagramming/lucidchart/free-plan/ ,
  https://pricingsaas.com/companies/lucidchart , https://costbench.com/changelog/lucidchart-price-decrease-2026-07/
- Guide copy rule: say "free plan limits documents and shapes", avoid exact numbers and prices.

## Our angle
No document or shape limits, no account, opens instantly, nothing uploaded, works offline, saves a file you own,
exports PNG/SVG/PDF with no watermark. Clean, simple UI with the 90% people use: flowcharts, org charts, ER.
Works on a phone (touch pan/zoom).

## Honest limits (do not claim)
- No real-time collaboration, comments, data linking, Visio import/export, AI.
- PDF export is an image of the diagram inside a PDF (not selectable vector text).
- Text uses the system font (Arial/Helvetica style), so exports look the same everywhere.
