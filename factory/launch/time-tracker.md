## AlternativeTo (suggest as alternative to Toggl Track)

**Name:** S3eidat Time Tracker
**Short description:** Free, private time tracker in the browser with billable rates, timesheet, reports and invoices.
**Description:**
Time Tracker is a one-person time tracker that runs entirely in your browser. Start a timer with a description,
project and tags, or add past entries with a duration like 1:30. Projects and clients have hourly rates, so reports
show billable hours and amounts, and you can turn a report into an invoice (print or save as PDF, or CSV). There is
a weekly timesheet, filters by client, project, tag and billable, detailed and summary CSV exports, rounding, and an
importer for Toggl Track detailed CSV exports. No account, nothing uploaded, works offline. No team features or sync.
**Tags:** time-tracking, timesheet, freelancer, invoicing, privacy, offline, no-signup
**License:** Free. **Platforms:** Web (any modern browser, desktop and mobile).

## Show HN

**Title:** Show HN: A browser-only time tracker with billable rates and invoices, no account

**First comment:**
I bill a few clients by the hour and wanted rates, billable totals and a simple invoice without a per-user
subscription. This is a static page: a timer whose state is just a start timestamp in localStorage (so it survives
closing the tab and stays in sync across tabs via the storage event), projects and clients with rates (project
overrides client overrides default), a weekly timesheet, reports with rounding, CSV exports, and an invoice that
uses the browser's print-to-PDF. It also imports Toggl Track's detailed CSV so you can bring your history.

No network calls (CSP connect-src 'self'), plain JS, no build step. Known gaps: no teams, no sync, no idle
detection. Happy to hear what a freelancer would need next.
