## AlternativeTo (suggest as alternative to PromptSmart)

**Name:** S3eidat Teleprompter
**Short description:** Free teleprompter in the browser with mirror mode, pedal keys and camera recording.
**Description:**
Paste a script and it scrolls smoothly past a reading line at the speed you set. Change speed and text size while
reading, jump back or forward with arrow or Page Up/Down keys (so most Bluetooth clickers and foot pedals work),
mirror the text for beam-splitter glass, and use a countdown. Turn on the camera to see yourself behind the script
and record a video. Several scripts, .txt open/save, cue lines and bold emphasis, Arabic right-to-left support.
No account and no uploads; works offline. It does not follow your voice.
**Tags:** teleprompter, autocue, video, public-speaking, privacy, offline, open-source
**License:** Free, open source. **Platforms:** Web (desktop, tablet, phone browsers).

## Show HN

**Title:** Show HN: A browser teleprompter with mirror mode and camera recording, no network calls

**First comment:**
I wanted a teleprompter I could open on any laptop or tablet without an app or account. It's one static page:
time-based scroll on a transform (smooth at any speed), live speed/size changes that keep your place, a reading-line
band, mirror/flip for prompter glass, and keys that Bluetooth page-turner pedals already send. Camera mode puts
getUserMedia video behind the text and records with MediaRecorder. It holds a screen wake lock while scrolling.
I left out voice-following on purpose: the Web Speech API sends audio to a server in most browsers, and the page
has a CSP with connect-src 'self'. Curious whether people have found an on-device way that works across browsers.
