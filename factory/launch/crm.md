## AlternativeTo (suggest as alternative to HubSpot CRM)

**Name:** S3eidat CRM
**Short description:** Free, private single-user CRM in the browser: contacts, deal pipeline, tasks, activity log.
**Description:**
A simple CRM for freelancers and small businesses that keeps everything on your own device. Contacts and companies,
a drag-and-drop deal pipeline with stage totals and win probabilities, tasks with due dates, and an activity log for
calls, emails, meetings and notes. Import contacts from a Google or Outlook CSV, export to CSV, and keep a backup
file. Supports many currencies including JOD. Free, no account, works offline; single user, no sync.

**First comment:**
I wanted a pipeline board and follow-up list for a small business without paying per seat or putting customer data
in someone's cloud. This is one static page: records in localStorage, a JSON backup file, CSV import that guesses
Google/Outlook column names, CSV export with a spreadsheet-formula guard. Deal board uses HTML5 drag and drop with a
stage select fallback for touch. It deliberately doesn't do teams or sync. I'd like to hear what a solo CRM can't
live without.
