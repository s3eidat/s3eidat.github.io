Facts you can say (all true in the code): free, no account, no limit on diagrams or shapes, no watermark, data
saved only in the browser, works offline after first load, 15 shapes (flowchart set, ER entity table, actor, note,
text), connectors that stay attached and re-route (elbow, straight, curved; arrow, open, diamond, circle, bar,
crow's foot ends; labels), quick-add connected shapes, snapping to grid and alignment guides, align/distribute,
undo/redo, copy/paste between tabs, templates (flowchart, org chart, ER), export PNG (transparent option), SVG,
PDF (image inside PDF), save/open .diagram.json, touch support (pinch zoom, double-tap to type).
Not true / don't claim: collaboration, comments, Visio import/export, auto-layout, obstacle-avoiding connectors,
vector PDF, AI, Arabic interface (Arabic text inside shapes works).

## AlternativeTo (suggest as alternative to Lucidchart)

**Name:** S3eidat Diagrams
**Short description:** Free, private flowchart and diagram maker in the browser. No limits, no sign-up, PNG/SVG/PDF export.
**Description:**
Diagrams is a browser-based editor for flowcharts, org charts and ER diagrams. Drag shapes from the library,
connect them by dragging from a connection dot, and connectors stay attached and re-route as you move things.
Elbow, straight or curved lines with arrows, crow's foot ends and labels; snapping to the grid and to other shapes;
align and distribute; quick-add a connected next step. Templates for a flowchart, an org chart and an ER diagram.
Export PNG (optionally transparent), SVG or PDF with no watermark, and save an editable file. There is no account
and no limit on documents or shapes; everything stays in your browser and it works offline. No real-time
collaboration.
**Tags:** flowchart, diagram, org-chart, er-diagram, privacy, offline, no-signup
**License:** Free. **Platforms:** Web (any modern browser, desktop and mobile).

## Show HN

**Title:** Show HN: A flowchart/diagram editor that runs entirely in the browser, no account, no limits

**First comment:**
I wanted a quick way to draw flowcharts and ER sketches without a free plan that stops at a few documents. This
is one static page: SVG canvas, shapes, connectors attached to shapes (elbow routing between connection points,
straight or curved), snapping to the grid and to other shapes' edges and centres, align/distribute, undo, and
templates. Export goes SVG → canvas → PNG, and the PDF is a tiny hand-written PDF wrapping a JPEG of the canvas
(so it isn't vector; SVG is). Touch works: pinch, one-finger pan, double-tap to edit.

No network calls (CSP connect-src 'self'), data in localStorage plus a JSON file you can save. Plain JS, no
build step. Known gaps: connectors don't route around obstacles, no groups or swimlanes yet, no collaboration.
Feedback on what's missing for your diagrams is welcome.
