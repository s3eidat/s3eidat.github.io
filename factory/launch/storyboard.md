## AlternativeTo (suggest as alternative to Boords)

**Name:** S3eidat Storyboard
**Short description:** Free storyboard maker in the browser: sketch or drop images, play an animatic, export PDF.
**Description:**
A storyboard tool that runs entirely in your browser. Sketch in each frame (pen, eraser, colours, stylus
pressure, rule-of-thirds grid) or drop in photos, then add shot type, camera move, duration, action, dialogue and
notes. Drag frames to reorder, watch the animatic with dialogue captions, and export a PDF (A4 or Letter, 1–4 frames
per row), a WebM video of the animatic, a CSV shot list or PNG frames. No account, no watermark, no uploads: work is
saved in the browser and can be saved as a project file. No collaboration or share links.
**Tags:** storyboard, animatic, filmmaking, shot-list, privacy, offline, open-source
**License:** Free, open source. **Platforms:** Web (any modern browser, desktop and tablet).

## Show HN

**Title:** Show HN: Free storyboard maker with animatic and PDF export, all client-side

**First comment:**
A storyboard tool as one static page: frames you can sketch in (pointer events, pressure) or fill with images,
shot/camera/duration/action/dialogue fields, drag to reorder, an animatic player, and exports. Some details that
might interest people here: the PDF is written by ~60 lines of JS (each page is rendered on a canvas and embedded
as a JPEG XObject), which is how Arabic and other complex scripts come out right without shipping fonts; the video
export records a canvas with MediaRecorder; projects live in IndexedDB. No network requests at all.
Known gaps: no collaboration, no audio track, WebM only, PDF text isn't selectable. Feedback welcome.
