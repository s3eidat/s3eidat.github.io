## Show HN

**Title:** Show HN: A free HTML email builder that runs entirely in the browser (no export caps)

**First comment:**
Template builders tend to cap free exports. This one turns a list of blocks into table-based HTML with inline styles,
an MSO wrapper and a small media query, and lets you copy or download it as often as you like. The preview renders
the real output inside a shadow root (so the email's CSS doesn't leak into the app) and deliberately doesn't fetch
remote images, since the page has no network access at all. Rich text goes through a whitelist sanitiser (b, i, u,
p, br, http/mailto/tel links). Feedback from people who fight Outlook for a living is welcome.
