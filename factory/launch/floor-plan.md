## AlternativeTo (suggest as alternative to RoomSketcher)

**Name:** S3eidat Floor Plan
**Short description:** Free 2D floor plan maker in the browser with room areas and PDF export to scale.
**Description:**
Draw walls (lengths shown as you draw), rooms with automatic areas in m² or ft², doors, windows and furniture on a
snapping grid. Edit exact wall lengths, reshape rooms by their corners, and export a PNG or a PDF at a real scale
(1:50, 1:100…) on A4, A3 or Letter with a scale bar. Plans are saved in your browser and as a file you can reopen.
Free, no account, no uploads. 2D only.

## Show HN

**Title:** Show HN: A 2D floor plan editor in one static page, with PDF export to scale

**First comment:**
I wanted to sketch my flat with real dimensions without an account or a paywall on 2D export. It's an SVG editor
in centimetre world units: walls snap to the grid, other wall ends and 15° angles; joined ends move together; rooms
are polygons whose area comes from the shoelace formula; doors/windows are parametrised along their wall so they
follow it. The PDF export rasterises the plan at the chosen scale (150 dpi) and writes a one-page PDF with a small
hand-written writer, so a 1:50 print really measures 2 cm per metre. 2D only; feedback on the editing feel welcome.
