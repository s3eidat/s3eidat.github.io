## AlternativeTo (suggest as alternative to MindMeister)

**Name:** S3eidat Mind Map
**Short description:** Free, keyboard-first mind map maker in the browser with PNG, SVG, PDF and Markdown export.
**Description:**
Mind Map is a fast mind mapping tool that runs in your browser. Press Tab for a subtopic and Enter for the next
topic, and the map lays itself out left and right of the centre. Drag topics to move them, fold branches, add
colours, icons and notes, and switch between light, dark and colourful styles. Export a sharp PNG, an SVG, a PDF
or a Markdown outline, or save the map as a file. It opens FreeMind (.mm) files and plain text outlines. There is
no account and no map limit; maps stay in your browser and it works offline. No collaboration yet.
**Tags:** mind-map, brainstorming, outline, note-taking, privacy, offline, no-signup
**License:** Free. **Platforms:** Web (any modern browser, desktop and mobile).

## Show HN

**Title:** Show HN: A keyboard-first mind map maker that runs entirely in your browser

**First comment:**
Mind Map is a static page: an SVG mind map with a balanced two-sided tree layout, keyboard editing (Tab, Enter,
arrows, Space to fold, Alt+arrows to reorder), drag to reparent, and pinch/Ctrl+scroll zoom. Exports are done on
the page: SVG via XMLSerializer, PNG through a canvas, a tiny hand-written PDF writer around a JPEG, and Markdown
outlines that import back (notes included). It also reads FreeMind .mm files.

No network calls (CSP connect-src 'self'), plain JS, no build. Known gaps: no collaboration, cross-links, free
topics or images in topics. I'd like to know which of those you'd miss first.

Disclosure: I made this. It's free, there's no account, and your maps stay on your device.
