## Show HN

**Title:** Show HN: A study planner that builds your revision plan, runs entirely in the browser

**First comment:**
I made a student planner as a static page: timetable, tasks, exams, and the part I always did badly by hand,
a revision plan. For each exam you list topics and pick easy/medium/hard; you set how many minutes you can study
on each weekday. It then spreads sessions between today and each exam (nearest exam first, a final review as close
to the exam as your time allows, never more than your daily minutes) and warns you when something doesn't fit.
Ticked sessions are kept; "Re-plan" rebuilds the rest after you miss a day.

No accounts, no network calls (CSP connect-src 'self'), data in localStorage with a JSON backup file, .ics export
for calendars, A/B week rotation, Arabic RTL UI. Plain JS, no build step. Known gaps: no sync, no notifications.
Feedback on the scheduling heuristic is very welcome.

**Title:** I made a free study planner that turns your exam dates into a day-by-day revision plan (no sign-up)

**Body:**
Disclosure: I built this. It's free, no account, and everything stays in your browser.

You add your subjects, weekly classes, homework and exams (with the topics you need to revise). Then you tell it
how many minutes you can study each day. It spreads revision sessions from today to each exam, does the closest
exam first, and puts a final review just before it. If you skip a day, "Re-plan" moves the missed work forward.

It also does A/B week timetables, exports to Google/Apple Calendar (.ics), and has an Arabic version.
What it doesn't do: sync between phone and laptop (there's a backup file instead) or send notifications.
