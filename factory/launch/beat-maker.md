## AlternativeTo (suggest as alternative to FL Studio — position as a lightweight starter)

**Name:** S3eidat Beat Maker
**Short description:** Free browser drum machine with synths, piano roll, song mode and WAV export.
**Description:**
A simple beat maker that runs in the browser with nothing to install: a 16-step drum sequencer with eight
synthesised drums (three kits), a bass and a lead synth with a piano roll, tempo and swing, eight patterns chained
into a song, and fast WAV export. Sounds are generated live by the Web Audio API, so there are no samples and no
uploads. Good for learning and quick ideas; it is not a full DAW (no recording, plugins or effects).
**Tags:** beat-maker, drum-machine, step-sequencer, music-production, web-audio, privacy, open-source
**License:** Free, open source. **Platforms:** Web (desktop and mobile).

## Show HN

**Title:** Show HN: A beat maker where every sound is synthesised in the browser (no samples)

**First comment:**
Drums are oscillators and filtered noise (pitch-dropping sine kick, noise snare with a triangle body, three-burst
clap, hats with choke), the bass and lead are simple subtractive synths, and playback uses the usual lookahead
scheduler on the AudioContext clock. WAV export builds the same graph in an OfflineAudioContext, so it renders faster
than real time and matches what you hear. No samples means nothing to license or download; the whole app is a static
page. It's deliberately small: 16 steps, 8 patterns, a song chain. Curious what people would add first.
