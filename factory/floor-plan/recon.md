# Recon: Floor Plan (free alternative to RoomSketcher)

Light recon, public pages only (run 2026-10-29). No RoomSketcher code, symbols, furniture art or copy reused.

## What the paid app is
Floor plan and home design app: draw walls and rooms, doors and windows, furniture library, measurements and room
areas, 2D and 3D views, export/print floor plans, ordering plans drawn by their team.
Sources: https://help.roomsketcher.com/hc/en-us/articles/21074497910045-Premium-vs-Pro-Feature-Comparison ,
https://www.roomsketcher.com/pro/ , https://www.toptenreviews.com/roomsketcher-review

## Pricing
Free tier to try; 2D floor plans need a paid per-project plan or a Pro subscription; ordering plans costs extra
(official pages above, third-party numbers vary). Guide copy rule: "paid plans", no exact prices.

## What users complain about
- Paywalls and extra charges; the free version can't even export 2D floor plans
  (https://www.toptenreviews.com/roomsketcher-review).
- Opaque pricing that keeps asking for a card (same review); frustration with credits/limits
  (https://www.capterra.com.au/software/164021/roomsketcher).

## Our angle
Free 2D floor plans in the browser: walls with live lengths, rooms with automatic m² (or ft²), doors, windows,
furniture, export PNG and a PDF at a real scale (1:50, 1:100…). No account, nothing uploaded, works offline.

## Honest limits (do not claim)
- 2D only, no 3D views or photoreal renders.
- Room areas come from the room shapes you draw (inner outline), not from automatic wall detection.
- Not a CAD/BIM tool; no DWG/DXF export.
