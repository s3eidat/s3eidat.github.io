# Recon: Beat Maker (free alternative to FL Studio, for beats)

Light recon, public pages only (run 2026-10-29). No Image-Line code, samples, sounds, skins or copy reused.

## What the paid app is
Full desktop DAW. The parts beginners use first: the Channel Rack step sequencer (drum patterns), the piano roll
(melodies and bass), patterns arranged in a playlist into a song, then export to WAV/MP3.
Sources: https://www.audeobox.com/learn/fl-studio/fl-studio-pricing-which-edition/ ,
https://www.getapp.com/audio-editing-software/a/fl-studio , https://producersphere.com/is-fl-studio-free

## Pricing
Paid editions (one-time price, lifetime updates); the cheapest edition limits the playlist and has no audio
recording; the trial can't reopen saved projects (third-party summaries above). Guide copy: "paid editions".

## What users complain about
- Steep learning curve for beginners (GetApp/Capterra reviews: https://www.capterra.ca/reviews/214598/fl-studio).
- Cost feels high for a first try; the cheapest edition lacks features beginners expect
  (https://www.audeobox.com/learn/compare/fl-studio-pricing-which-edition/).
- Desktop install needed; school/shared computers often can't install it.

## Our angle
A simple beat maker that opens in the browser: drum step sequencer with synthesized drums (no samples to download),
bass and lead synths with a piano roll, tempo and swing, patterns chained into a song, WAV export. Free, no account,
no install, works offline. A first step, not a DAW.

## Honest limits (do not claim)
- Not a DAW: no audio recording, no plugins/VST, no mixer effects chain beyond simple volume/filter, no MIDI export
  in core (could), sounds are synthesized (not sampled instruments).
- Timing depends on the browser's audio clock; heavy tabs can stutter on slow phones.
