# Recon: Mind Map (free alternative to MindMeister)

Light recon, public pages only (run 2026-10-29). Nothing from MindMeister (code, logo, themes, icons, copy) is
reused. Generic terms only (topic, branch, subtopic, outline).

## What the paid app is
Online mind mapping: central topic, branches, keyboard shortcuts (Tab child, Enter sibling), drag to reorganise,
colours and themes, icons, notes, links, images, collapse, outline view, presentation mode, collaboration,
export (PDF, PNG, Word, PowerPoint, outline), import (FreeMind, XMind, outline text).

## Pricing (secondary sources; verify on the vendor page before quoting a number)
- Free Basic plan limited to 3 mind maps (sources disagree on per-user vs per-team and on private maps).
  Paid Personal / Pro / Business about €6.50 / €10.50 / €15.50 per user per month.
  Sources: https://www.capterra.com/p/267319/MindMeister/ , https://toolradar.com/tools/mindmeister/pricing ,
  https://frontdeskreview.com/software/mind-mapping/mindmeister/ , https://www.stork.ai/en/mindmeister
- Guide copy rule: say "the free plan limits how many maps you can keep", avoid exact numbers and prices.

## What users complain about
- The 3-map limit and features behind the subscription (Capterra reviews above; GetApp:
  https://www.getapp.com/mind-mapping-software/a/mindmeister).
- Lag with large maps (stork.ai above).
- Free alternatives people name: Coggle (limited private maps), desktop Freeplane/XMind
  (https://toolradar.com/guides/best-free-mind-mapping-software).

## Our angle
Unlimited maps, no account, opens instantly, keyboard-first, works offline and on a phone, export PNG/SVG/PDF
and a Markdown outline, import an outline or a FreeMind .mm file. Arabic topics read right to left.

## Honest limits (do not claim)
- No collaboration, sharing links, presentation mode, images inside topics or Word/PowerPoint export.
- PDF export is an image of the map in a PDF.
