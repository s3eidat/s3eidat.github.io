# Recon: Teleprompter (free alternative to PromptSmart)

Light recon, public pages only (run 2026-10-29). No PromptSmart code, branding, icons or copy reused.

## What the paid app is
Mobile/desktop teleprompter: script library, smooth scroll with speed control, font size, mirror mode for
beam-splitter glass, countdown, Bluetooth remote/pedal control, record video with the script on screen, and
"VoiceTrack" speech-recognition scrolling that follows your voice.
Sources: https://apps.apple.com/app/id894811756 , https://apps.apple.com/app/id1526733375 ,
https://teleprompter.com/compare/teleprompter-vs-promptsmart

## Pricing
Pro: paid upfront plus an optional "Extended" subscription for remote/sync; PromptSmart+: free download, voice
tracking needs a subscription after a trial (App Store listings above). Guide copy rule: "paid app / subscription",
no exact prices.

## What users complain about
- Paying once, then needing a subscription for full features (https://teleprompter.com/compare/teleprompter-vs-promptsmart).
- Voice tracking stops working or doesn't understand accents (https://appsumo.com/products/promptsmart/reviews/).
- Charges after trials (aggregated app review summaries; treat with caution).
- Crashes/freezing reported (comparison above).

## Our angle
Free, no account, works in any browser on laptop, tablet or phone, offline, scripts stay on the device.
Smooth adjustable scroll, mirror, countdown, keyboard and Bluetooth page-turner control, reading line, Arabic
right-to-left scripts, camera recording with the script on screen, all free.

## Honest limits (do not claim)
- No voice-follow scrolling: browser speech recognition sends audio to a server in most browsers, which breaks our
  "nothing leaves your device" promise. Speed control + keys/pedal instead.
- No phone-as-remote over Wi-Fi (needs a network). Bluetooth keyboards/pedals that send arrow or page keys work.
- The recording is the camera video only; the script is not burned into it.
