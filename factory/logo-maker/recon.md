# Recon: Logo Maker (free alternative to Looka)

Light recon, public pages only (run 2026-10-29). No Looka code, branding, icons, templates, fonts or copy reused.

## What the paid app is
Logo generator: enter a business name and industry, pick styles/colours/symbols you like, get many generated logo
ideas, edit layout/fonts/colours/symbol, then pay to download. Brand Kit subscription adds social media kits,
business cards, brand guidelines, website.
Sources: https://www.techradar.com/pro/what-is-looka-everything-we-know-about-the-ai-logo-and-brand-building-tool ,
https://costbench.com/software/ai-design-tools/looka/ , https://ecomm.design/looka-review/

## Pricing
Free to design and preview; paying is required to download usable files. One-time logo packages (PNG-only tier,
vector tier) and yearly Brand Kit subscriptions (third-party summaries above, numbers vary).
Guide copy rule: "free to try, pay to download", no exact prices.

## What users complain about
- You only find out at the end that downloading costs money; vector files cost more than PNG
  (https://ecomm.design/looka-review/ , https://www.nichepursuits.com/looka-review/).
- Confusing package/pricing structure; low-resolution files on the cheapest tier
  (https://softwarefinder.com/marketing-software/looka-ai — thin evidence).
- Brand kit is a yearly subscription (https://costbench.com/software/ai-design-tools/looka/).

## Our angle
Free download of everything: SVG (vector), transparent PNG in any size, favicon sizes, and a brand-sheet PNG with
colours and variants. No account, nothing uploaded. Many ideas from a name + industry, then a simple editor.

## Honest limits (do not claim)
- Not AI. Ideas are combinations of our own icon set, layouts, palettes and type styles.
- One bundled typeface family (Inter, in many weights) plus the device's serif/mono fonts; SVG keeps the text as
  live text, so it needs the font installed (Inter is free) to look identical elsewhere. PNG is exact.
- No trademark search: a generated logo can resemble others; users should check before registering.
