Privacy

Last updated 26 October 2026

The short version: I don't collect anything. These tools have no accounts, no servers that receive your files, and no analytics or tracking scripts.

Your files and text

Everything you put into a tool, whether PDFs, photos, text or passwords, is processed by your own browser on your own device. It is never uploaded. Every page carries a Content-Security-Policy with connect-src 'self', which means the browser itself blocks the page from sending data to any other website.

Saved settings

Some tools remember your settings or your own content (notes, to-dos, invoices) in your browser's local storage, so they are still there next time. That data stays on your device. Clearing your browser data deletes it, and I can't see it.

Hosting

The pages are static files hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may keep standard server logs (for example IP addresses) for security. See GitHub's privacy statement for details. I don't receive those logs.

Coffee payments

If you buy me a coffee, the payment happens on Stripe's own checkout page. Stripe processes your payment details under its privacy policy. I receive the amount, your email for the receipt, and any note you choose to leave. I never see your card.

Promotions

Some pages show a small, clearly labelled promotion for another product. It is a plain link with no tracking pixels and no third-party ad scripts.

Open source

Every tool is open source on GitHub, so you can check all of this yourself.
